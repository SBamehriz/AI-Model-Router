# Licensing

AI Model Router uses [PolyForm Shield 1.0.0](../LICENSE). It permits personal use, study, modification, and internal commercial use, subject to its terms.

The noncompete restriction applies to providing a product or service that competes with the software or another product the licensor provides using it. Reselling a competing router or offering it as a hosted service requires separate permission. Free competing offerings are also restricted. Unrelated commercial use is not generally prohibited.

This is source available software. It is not OSI open source because it restricts competing uses. Describing it as unrestricted open source would misrepresent those permissions.

## Why Shield

Shield is closer to allowing free use while restricting a competing resale or hosted service than PolyForm Noncommercial, which also excludes internal commercial work. Shield has no scheduled conversion to a permissive license. Its specific rules for new products and discontinued products still apply.

The official license controls. This page summarizes it and does not add restrictions or grant extra rights. See the [PolyForm Project text](https://polyformproject.org/licenses/shield/1.0.0). Contact [Salim Ba Mehriz](https://github.com/SBamehriz) to discuss separate permission.
